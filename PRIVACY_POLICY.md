# Privacy Policy for ShiftPay

**Effective Date:** August 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 26, 2026

**ShiftPay** ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application ("App").

---

## 1. Information Collection and Storage

### 1.1 Local Data Storage (Offline-First)
ShiftPay is designed as an offline-first tool. All data entered by you into the application—including shift hours, clock-in/out timestamps, base hourly wages, overtime rules, night differential configurations, notes, and shift history logs—is stored **locally on your device** via secure local storage. We do not transmit, sell, or store your personal wage records on external servers.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Diagnostic and Usage Data
To maintain application performance, fix bugs, and optimize user experience, we use third-party services that may collect pseudonymous device identifiers:

- **Google Analytics for Firebase**: Collects anonymous interaction metrics (e.g., screens viewed, buttons clicked, crash logs, and general operating system versions) to help us diagnose issues and improve application features.
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: Uses anonymous device identifiers and advertising IDs to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

## 3. Data Safety & Security

- **Encryption in Transit**: All analytics and advertising communications with Google servers utilize standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.
- **Data Deletion**: You can delete all your stored shifts and reset preferences at any time directly within the app or by clearing the app data in your device settings.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

ShiftPay is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
